# Athena — 总有一天

Mode: built-in image_gen, imagegen skill. Three original image-only scenic plates; final posters combine these plates with exact typesetting and unmodified Electron runtime screenshots.

User direction: 九张宣传海报，加入实机图；不要太抽象、太花哨或太写实。主题来自用户提供的「总有一天」诗段。

## clouds

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: image-only art plate for a premium Chinese Athena desktop-assistant promotional poster series themed "总有一天" (Someday), based on a poem about two travelers looking beyond clouds and moonlight, moving through night, pushing a boulder over a mountain, silver stars, and a return through snow. ONE portrait 3:4 image, ideally 1536 x 2048. This is a restrained modern poetic commercial poster image, semi-real cinematic editorial illustration with soft airbrushed painterly atmosphere and quiet fine paper grain. Recognizable landscape and human-scale journey, not an abstract sculpture and not a tourist photograph. Carefully edited shapes, monumental scale, minimal detail, spacious composition, emotionally stoic and unresolved, no triumphant sunrise. No text or logos anywhere. No app screens, no lettering, no watermark, no frame. Leave upper-left 40 percent simple for later exact typesetting; keep the most interesting visual elements at edges and lower third so a real app screenshot can be positioned over the middle in product variants. All images in this campaign share slate blue, blue-grey, charcoal, cold white and understated silver. No neon, orange gradients, fluorescent outlines, fantasy magic, decorative particles, busy animals, photoreal close-up people, glossy glass blobs, detailed science fiction or generic AI iconography. Specific scene: Beyond the clouds. Vast quiet blue-grey sky, an immense pale cloud ocean spilling between dark slate cliffs; a thin rocky path appears along the bottom right then disappears into luminous low cloud. Two very small anonymous travelers, no bigger than 2 percent of image height, stand together on the distant ridge in the lower right looking toward open cloud space. No visible faces. Their silhouette should be a moving human detail, not a heroic action pose. Upper sky stays softly dark blue-grey, mid distance airy silver clouds, foreground deep desaturated slate. One graceful narrow beam of cool daylight through cloud, very subtle. Use beautifully designed large atmospheric shapes and generous uninterrupted space, editorial not a fantasy game loading screen.
```
