## Prompt

+Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: Athena master visual system direction board.

Create a premium 3-by-2 brand visual-system board for “Athena”, a calm personal AI companion inspired by wisdom, clarity, and long-term companionship.

Input roles:
- Image 1 is the authoritative Athena logo geometry: crescent, classical temple, feather, two stars. Do not redesign it.
- Image 2 is the approved positive/negative colorway and title-case Athena wordmark. Use it as the color and identity anchor.

Core visual idea: “Open Field of Wisdom”. Extend the logo into a restrained visual language without repeating the full logo everywhere. Derive three separate graphic behaviors:
1. OPEN ARC — one oversized cropped crescent-like arc creating an open protective boundary and generous empty center.
2. ORDER — five quiet vertical bands or hairlines derived from temple rhythm, contemporary and abstract, never a literal building.
3. FLOW — one broad, softly tapering diagonal curve derived from the feather’s gesture, with no feather barbs.
Each application panel must use only ONE of these behaviors as its dominant device. Never stack all three as decorative clutter.

Layout: sophisticated 3×2 editorial grid on a dark charcoal outer canvas, precise gutters, strong hierarchy, generous whitespace.
Panel 1, identity cover: warm ivory field, approved Athena logo and exact title-case wordmark “Athena”, centered and calm.
Panel 2, primary key visual: large warm ivory field with one monumental cropped sage open arc entering from the lower-left edge, a small Athena wordmark placed in negative space, one tiny champagne-gold star accent maximum.
Panel 3, order system: pale stone field with five very subtle vertical sage bands, large empty area, tiny logo in one corner.
Panel 4, flow system: deep sage field with one ivory softly tapering diagonal curve crossing from lower-left toward upper-right, no feather details, small champagne accent at the endpoint.
Panel 5, material/color system: four large clean swatches or material planes—warm ivory #F5F1E8, deep sage #53675F, graphite #222C2A, muted champagne #B49A6A—quiet flat-to-matte surfaces with only extremely subtle mineral grain.
Panel 6, brand application: one refined editorial poster or product welcome card using a single oversized open arc crop, small approved logo, exact word “Athena”, and the exact short phrase “与你一起思考”. No software interface and no device mockup.

Visual mood: warm daylight, mineral stone, soft natural side light, calm professional hierarchy, companion warmth, modern editorial minimalism, subtle depth. Primarily flat fields with very restrained paper/mineral texture at edges only. The result should feel like a serious design studio presentation, not a luxury hotel, mythology museum, tarot brand, wedding brand, or AI template.

Typography: only use the exact words “Athena” and “与你一起思考”; no other text, captions, numbers, labels, lorem ipsum, or tiny fake copy. Athena in refined title-case serif; Chinese phrase in clean restrained sans serif.

Strict constraints:
- No IP character, goddess portrait, human figure, face, avatar, or silhouette.
- No software UI, dashboard, chat bubbles, browser chrome, phone, laptop, buttons or controls.
- Do not draw additional temples, columns, helmets, owls, laurel wreaths, leaves, moons, feathers, stars, sparkles or mythological objects outside the approved full logo.
- The open arc is a brand-derived abstract curve, not a literal moon.
- The order bands are abstract alignment rhythm, not literal columns.
- The flow curve is one smooth stroke, not a feather illustration.
- No purple-blue AI gradients, neon, glassmorphism, glowing orb, particles, galaxy or circuitry.
- Keep the approved logo geometry consistent wherever it appears.
- Preserve a restrained palette; champagne gold never exceeds 8 percent of any panel.
