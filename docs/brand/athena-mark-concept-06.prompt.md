## Prompt

+Use case: logo-brand.
Design one exceptionally refined minimal symbol for ATHENA, a local personal AI assistant inspired by Athena, the Greek goddess of wisdom.

The approved concept: an abstract ancient Greek helmet fused with a capital A in negative space. User insists it must be beautiful, simple, poised, and NOT rigid or stiff. Previous overly decorated temple emblems and generic serif A glyphs were rejected.

Create a distinctive, fluid silhouette using at most two or three substantial flat shapes. A gently swept-back helmet crown creates an elegant asymmetric curve; a restrained angular opening and cheek-guard relationship subtly suggest the letter A through empty space. The A should be discovered, not pasted on. Use the tension of one soft long curve and one crisp diagonal. Open breathing room, moderate stroke weight, subtly rounded transitions, balanced confident proportions. Small enough to work as an app identity. Evoke Athena through the abstract helmet object alone: no human face, no eye, no head illustration. It should feel intelligent and approachable, not martial, aggressive, sporty, regal or antique. Avoid spiky crests; suggest the sweep with one continuous curve instead of drawing plumes. Not a frontal symmetric mask. Do not resemble a Spartan esports team or a football helmet.

Make a quiet, beautifully composed landscape concept sheet on a completely OPAQUE warm ivory background. One large deep navy symbol occupies the left two-thirds, with the small exact word ATHENA beneath in refined, clean uppercase typography. On the right, show precisely the same symbol in one small flat rounded-square midnight-navy app tile, rendered ivory. Plenty of empty space. Only two presentations of the SAME logo, not two different designs.
Entirely flat solid fills. No gradients, textures, shiny metals, shadows, embossing, construction lines, explanatory labels or extra text.
No separate A letter inside a helmet, no circles, no shields, no borders, no columns, temples, stars, moons, lightning, wings, laurel wreaths, feathers, owl, goddess character or IP portrait.
Prioritize an original elegant contour and generous meaningful negative space. Not a generic lettermark. Output a polished design proposal, not a sketch.
