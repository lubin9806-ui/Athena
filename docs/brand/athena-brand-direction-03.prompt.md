## 完整提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one refined, original ATHENA brand identity and app-icon concept board from the supplied references.

Input roles:
- Images 1, 2, 3: the user's hand-drawn structural thinking. Treat these as the most important reference: a classical pediment/temple roof, an enclosing crescent-like arc, a central monogram/path, one spark, and geometric construction.
- Images 4, 5, 6, 7, 8: visual-quality, gold/ivory/midnight material, presentation, and application references only. Do not copy their exact logos, layouts, text, feather motif, or Athena Muse naming.

Brand context: ATHENA is a local-first personal AI assistant inspired by the Greek goddess of wisdom. It helps the user clarify questions, compare possibilities, improve ideas, and move from thought to action. The mark needs a story and must work as a Windows desktop app icon. It is not a Muse product.

Design one coherent symbol, not multiple alternatives:
1. A simplified classical pediment provides the upper silhouette and represents Athena, wisdom and order.
2. A broad open arc surrounds the structure without closing into a full circle. This is a brand-specific symbol for an assistant that remains nearby across day and night; do not claim the crescent is a historical Athena symbol.
3. A custom geometric A is integrated into the architecture, not placed on top. Its two legs also form an open forward path. The lower right leg resolves into one restrained angular action cut, derived from the user's lightning sketch, expressing thought becoming action. It must not look like a generic lightning-bolt tech logo.
4. One four-point spark sits in the negative-space counter, expressing the moment a problem becomes clear.
5. No columns thinner than the minimum stroke; use at most three simplified vertical architectural strokes in the master mark, and remove them in smaller variants.

The final emblem must feel elegant, intelligent, calm and mythic, with strong balanced geometry. It must be recognizably related to the user's sketch yet visibly redrawn and reduced. Avoid a busy seal, literal five-column temple, feather, owl, leaf, goddess face, helmet, shield, laurels, character illustration, magic effects, decorative star field, or copied existing brand. No IP character inside UI applications.

Board design:
- Landscape 3:2, high resolution, warm ivory #F7F3EA background, deep midnight #111C29 text, antique gold #C99A4B primary mark, stone #CFC5B5 secondary. Premium identity studio board, generous negative space, crisp typography.
- Header exact: "ATHENA" and "智慧，从思考走向行动"
- Left 43%: very large flat master emblem in antique gold, with wordmark "ATHENA" below. Flat vector-like rendering, no 3D bevel in the master.
- Center 25%: a clear four-step construction story with exact short labels:
  "神殿屋顶 · 智慧与秩序"
  "开放弧光 · 昼夜相伴"
  "字母路径 · 从思考到行动"
  "星芒内核 · 问题变清晰"
  Include a light geometric construction overlay showing circle/triangle proportions but no fake mathematical measurements.
- Right 32%: adaptive application system. Show:
  a full master mark at 128px;
  a simplified app mark at 64/48px with roof + A/path + open arc + spark and no thin columns;
  a micro mark at 32/16px with only the bold A/path + partial arc, no spark if illegible;
  monochrome midnight-on-ivory and ivory-on-midnight;
  one ivory Windows rounded-square tile and one midnight tile using the exact same simplified app mark;
  one tiny real-looking application titlebar fragment with the micro mark + ATHENA, memory icon, settings icon, minimize and close;
  no person, no avatar, no portrait.
- Footer exact: "概念方向 03 · 主视觉与软件图标共用同一故事"

Make the main mark readable and ownable. The user should immediately see the architectural wisdom origin, then discover the companion arc, thought-to-action path and clarity spark. All Chinese text sparse and legible. Do not include "Muse", "灵感女神", marketing chat mockups, unrelated UI dashboards, or any character art.
