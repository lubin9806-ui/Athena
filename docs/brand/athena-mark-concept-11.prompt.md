## Prompt

+Design a beautiful, extremely simple original logo for ATHENA, a thoughtful personal AI companion. Fresh concept, NOT a revision of a helmet. The entire identity is a single graceful custom A-shaped gesture.

Visual design: two gently tapering rising strokes meet in a soft, slightly off-center apex. A single fluid transverse cut through their lower-middle creates the A recognition and a subtle sense of openness. The inner counter is generous, clean and softly triangular. The right stroke is lighter and turns gently outward at its base, giving the otherwise stable symbol a quiet living asymmetry. Precisely balanced medium-weight silhouette, elegant continuous curves, restrained rounded transitions. NOT a thick inverted U, doorway, puffy arch, ordinary font letter, infinity loop, ribbon knot or Airbnb-style heart loop. No detached dots or arbitrary holes. No razor-sharp ends. Keep all parts optically coherent, readable as one compact symbol with one memorable asymmetry.

The Greek origin is expressed by a contemporary alpha-like structure only. Calm wisdom and warm companionship, not military, fantasy, security or luxury hotel. Mature, understated, distinct. Do not copy any famous logo.

Presentation MUST be clearly visible: flat dark charcoal mark (#252827) on an entirely filled light warm-gray background (#E6E3DE). The background is part of the artwork, not transparency; fill every pixel outside the mark, including its counter, with light warm gray. Square composition. Main symbol occupies about 40 percent of canvas width, centered slightly above middle with very generous breathing room. Small exact word ATHENA beneath in refined simple uppercase sans serif. No app tile, extra variants, construction grids, labels, borders or decorative elements. No texture, gradients, lighting effects or shadows. Clean solid-color graphic artwork.
Absolutely no helmet, eye slit, feather, crest, spiral, swoosh, temple, column, moon, star, shield, heart, owl, leaves, face, robot, figure, or random cutout. Beauty must come from the proportions of the one silhouette.
