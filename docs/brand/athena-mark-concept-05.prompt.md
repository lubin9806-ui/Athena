## Prompt

+Use case: logo-brand.
Create one refined minimalist logo concept presentation for ATHENA, a personal AI thinking assistant named after the Greek goddess of wisdom.
The user's strongest requirement: combine Greek cultural meaning with the name Athena in a SIMPLE, poised, beautiful mark. This is a radical reduction, NOT a decorated mythological badge.

Design exactly ONE custom A monogram. Fuse the structural proportions of a Greek temple pediment into the letter itself: the two sloping strokes of the A ARE the architectural summit, and a calm horizontal crossbar subtly suggests an architrave. Do not draw a separate roof above an A. Broad stable stance, carefully tapered solid strokes, small restrained classical terminal details, one generous triangular counter, beautifully balanced positive and negative space. Contemporary architectural typography, quiet intelligence, dignity, not aggressive. The design should be recognizable as a deliberately crafted A, not a generic font glyph, not a literal temple illustration. All symbolic meaning must reside in this ONE cohesive silhouette.

Presentation: exceptionally sparse horizontal ivory canvas. Left two thirds: one large flat midnight-navy logo with generous clear space, beneath it the exact word ATHENA in elegant moderately spaced uppercase typography. Right third: two small flat rounded-square app tiles, one navy with ivory mark, one ivory with subdued antique-gold mark. Reuse precisely the same silhouette, no variant logo designs. This is only a logo concept sheet, not a comprehensive brand board.
Flat solid colors, razor-sharp vector-like edges, professional optical balance, no lighting effects.
Avoid ALL crescents, enclosing circles, orbits, stars, sparkles, lightning bolts, feathers, leaves, owls, shields, columns, faces, goddess figures, crests, separate roofs, ornamental borders, textures, embossing, metallic shading, gradients, drop shadows, construction grids, captions, paragraphs, UI screenshots, tiny fake labels.
Only text: ATHENA. Prioritize the beauty and simplicity of the symbol over everything else.
