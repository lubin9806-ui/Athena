# Athena 三图融合简化概念 09

用户要求：融合金色神殿羽毛图、柱式竖琴草图与柔化头盔 A 图，简洁大气美观。

采用选择性融合：保留 A 与上方舒展轮廓，以柱头/琴臂卷曲替换锐利护面，羽毛仅提取外轮廓走势。不叠加神殿、月牙、星芒、羽毛细节或人物。

生成方式：brandkit 构思约束，imagegen 内置工具，三张用户图片作为视觉参考。品牌仍为 ATHENA，未采用参考图中的 MUSE 名字。

视觉检查：结果主要由三块形状构成，护面改为卷曲，三角与留白保留 A 联想。顶部与左下仍有渐尖收尾，需用户判断柔和程度。小图与大图局部比例仍需后续矢量统一；未进行小尺寸及商标验证。仅概念图，未替换软件资源。

## Prompt

+Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE new, dramatically simplified ATHENA logo by intelligently fusing the three reference images, not by stacking their symbols. ATHENA is a gentle personal companion AI inspired by Greek wisdom, not a military or gaming brand.

Reference roles:
1. Gold MUSE temple/feather image: borrow ONLY the light flowing feather gesture and classical balance, not its elaborate decoration or MUSE name.
2. Black column/lyre sketch: borrow the gentle S-shaped lyre arm, one open Ionic scroll, and the poised vertical rhythm. Ignore the screenshot UI/checkerboard completely.
3. Sage ATHENA helmet/A concept: preserve the capital A recognition and the sweeping upper silhouette, but meaningfully redesign the severe face guard and oversized helmet crown.

Design synthesis: a compact flowing A monogram in which the left diagonal sweeps upward into a low feather-like crown, and the right leg is a gently curling lyre arm, NOT a pointed mask. A single open inward curl at its upper end subtly recalls the Ionic capital and a lyre. Between the curved arms keep one generous triangular negative space to suggest A. At most TWO short parallel inner strokes can suggest lyre strings only if needed. This must read as ONE coherent elegant symbol: A first, Greek lyrical character second, abstract helmet third. Remove the literal angry eye slit entirely. The lyre curve REPLACES the cheek guard rather than sitting alongside a helmet. The soft crest has no feather barbs. No separate temple, no surrounding crescent, no separate feather. No lower swoosh or banner. Maximum three substantial major forms plus the optional two inner strokes. Bold enough for a small app icon, ample clear negative space, subtle asymmetry, beautifully tuned thick-to-thin curves, rounded gentle terminals. Not an ordinary typed A, not a detailed illustration.

Mood: quiet intelligence, warmth, listening and personal companionship. Graceful and confident, neither stiff nor overly cute. Modern Greek-inspired identity without imperial, warlike or luxury hotel crest styling.

Presentation: one large muted sage (#52675D) logo on the left of an OPAQUE warm ivory (#F7F4EE) horizontal canvas, small exact word ATHENA beneath in refined widely spaced clean uppercase lettering. One smaller flat sage rounded square app tile on the right with the EXACT same symbol in ivory. Wide quiet margins, no other panels. Entirely solid flat fills and crisp vector-like edges, NO gradients, lighting, metallic effect, texture, 3D, shadows or transparent background.
Avoid: stars, moons, shields, spear tips, angry eyes, human faces, characters, owl, leaves, temple pediments, columns, detailed strings, baroque coils, musical notes, multiple separate logos, text other than ATHENA. Keep the result simple, warm, spacious and beautiful.
